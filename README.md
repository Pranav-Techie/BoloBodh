# BoloBodh AI 📚🎙️
### Learn in Your Language. Ask Freely. Understand Better.

**BoloBodh AI** is a multilingual, voice-first AI learning assistant designed to make learning more accessible, interactive, and personalized for school students.

Students can ask academic questions in their preferred language, listen to spoken explanations, summarize study materials, generate practice quizzes, and revisit concepts through personalized revision support.

Built for the **Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI**, BoloBodh explores how Indian speech AI and conversational learning technology can help students overcome language barriers and make learning more accessible.

---

## 🌟 The Problem

Many students struggle when educational explanations and digital learning resources are not available in a language they understand comfortably. Reading lengthy study materials, asking questions confidently, and revising concepts can also be challenging.

Traditional digital learning tools may rely heavily on typing and reading, creating additional barriers for students who prefer speaking and listening.

## 💡 Our Solution

BoloBodh AI combines voice-first interaction, multilingual learning, and AI-assisted study tools in one student-friendly platform.

Students can ask questions, receive explanations, interact through speech, summarize PDF study materials, practice with quizzes, and revisit their learning activities through a unified dashboard.

Our goal is to make learning more natural, interactive, and accessible.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎙️ 1. Voice-First Learning

BoloBodh enables students to interact with the learning assistant using speech.

- Convert spoken questions into text using Gnani AI speech-to-text technology.
- Listen to AI-generated explanations through text-to-speech.
- Support a more natural question-and-answer experience.
- Help learners who prefer speaking and listening over typing and reading.

### 🌍 2. Multilingual Learning

Learn in a language that feels comfortable.

- **Hindi:** Ask questions and explore academic concepts in Hindi.
- **English:** Learn and practice in English.
- **Hinglish:** Interact using natural Hindi-English mixed language.

The application uses the student's language preference to support a more personalized learning experience. Language behavior and speech availability may vary depending on the selected language and the integrated AI services.

### 🤖 3. Ask a Doubt — AI Learning Assistant

The Ask a Doubt feature helps students explore academic topics through conversational explanations.

- Ask questions about school-level concepts.
- Receive AI-generated explanations.
- Explore difficult topics in simpler language.
- Continue learning by asking additional questions.
- Listen to spoken answers when voice output is available.

**Goal:** Make it easier for students to ask questions, understand concepts, and learn at their own pace.

### 📄 4. Smart PDF Summarization

Turn lengthy study material into concise revision-friendly content.

- Upload a supported PDF document.
- Process its readable content.
- Generate an AI-powered summary.
- Review key concepts without repeatedly reading the entire document.
- Use the summary as a starting point for revision.

**Why it matters:** Students can review lengthy chapters, notes, and study resources more efficiently.

Summary quality depends on document readability, text extraction, and the generated output.

### 📝 5. AI-Powered Practice Quiz

Test your understanding through generated practice questions.

- Generate quizzes using AI.
- Practice concepts covered in your learning material.
- Submit answers and receive feedback.
- View scores and identify topics that may need more practice.

**Why it matters:** Active practice helps students check their understanding instead of only reading or memorizing content.

### 📚 6. Revision Notes

The dedicated Revision Notes section helps students revisit learning content and organize revision activities.

- Access revision support through a separate section.
- Revisit important learning points.
- Keep revision activities organized.

### 📊 7. Quiz History

Continue learning from previous attempts.

- Review saved quiz attempts where available.
- Revisit previous scores and feedback.
- Identify concepts that need additional practice.
- Support continuity across learning sessions.

### 👤 8. Student Dashboard and Preferences

A unified dashboard brings the main learning tools together.

- Access doubt solving, study materials, and quizzes.
- Set language preferences.
- Navigate to revision notes and quiz history.
- Maintain a more personalized learning experience.

## 🧠 BoloBodh Learning Intelligence Engine (BLIE)

The **BoloBodh Learning Intelligence Engine (BLIE)** is the product-level name for the text-based learning intelligence component of BoloBodh AI.

It brings together AI-powered educational workflows to transform student questions and study materials into useful learning experiences.

### Core capabilities

- **Concept Explanation:** Generates conversational answers to academic questions.
- **Smart Study Summaries:** Converts readable PDF content into concise summaries.
- **Practice Question Generation:** Creates quizzes to help students test their understanding.
- **Learning Feedback:** Supports answer evaluation and feedback during practice.
- **Learning Continuity:** Works alongside revision and history features to help students revisit their learning activities.

BLIE works with the platform's backend and AI language model integration, while Gnani AI speech services enable voice-based interaction.

*BLIE is the name of BoloBodh's learning-intelligence component, not a claim that a new foundation model has been independently trained.*

## 🇮🇳 Built for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge

BoloBodh AI was developed for the **Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI**, focused on building practical applications using AI technologies relevant to India.

The project explores how multilingual speech technology and AI-assisted learning can make educational experiences more accessible to students.

### Gnani AI Integration

| Technology | Role in BoloBodh |
|---|---|
| **Gnani AI Prisma v2.5 — Speech-to-Text (STT)** | Converts spoken questions into text |
| **Gnani AI Timbre v2.5 — Text-to-Speech (TTS)** | Converts text responses into spoken audio |

These services connect speech input and spoken output with the platform's AI-assisted learning workflows.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Choose your language:** Set your preferred learning language.
2. **Ask a question:** Type a doubt or use voice interaction where supported.
3. **Get an explanation:** Receive an AI-generated answer.
4. **Listen and learn:** Use spoken output when available.
5. **Upload study material:** Submit a supported PDF for summarization.
6. **Practice:** Generate a quiz and answer the questions.
7. **Review:** Revisit revision notes and saved quiz history where available.

## 🔐 Privacy and Configuration

BoloBodh connects to external AI and database services through backend configuration.

- Store API keys and credentials in environment variables.
- Never commit `.env` files or secrets to GitHub.
- Configure required credentials separately for local development and deployment.
- Avoid uploading sensitive personal information or confidential documents.
